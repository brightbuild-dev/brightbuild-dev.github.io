1. What we collect

Overripe uses Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob, both part of Google. Between them, they automatically collect standard mobile analytics and advertising data: a device/advertising identifier, general device information (model, OS version), approximate location derived from IP address, and app usage events.

The usage events specific to this app are limited to gameplay statistics - nothing you type, nothing personal:

EventWhat it tells us
Run started / endedGame mode, score, turns played, how long the run lasted
Fruit harvestedHarvest size and value, sampled from a subset of runs
Retry tappedHow quickly you started a new run after one ended
Tutorial completedWhether the onboarding walkthrough was finished

We use this only to understand whether the game is fun and where players get stuck. We do not collect your name, email address, photos, contacts, or location beyond the coarse, IP-derived estimate Google's SDKs generate automatically.

2. What never leaves your device

Your best scores, run history, Daily Challenge results, and the "show numbers on fruit" setting are stored only in the app's local storage on your device (Android DataStore). We never see this data, and it is deleted the moment you uninstall the app.

3. Third-party services

Overripe has no server of its own. Every network request the app makes goes to one of the following, each governed by its own privacy policy:

ServiceUsed for
Firebase AnalyticsThe gameplay statistics described above
Google AdMobShowing the interstitial ad that appears after every third completed run, for players who haven't purchased ad removal
Google Play BillingProcessing the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase

All three are Google products, covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Play Billing purchases are handled entirely by Google Play - Overripe never sees your payment card details.

4. Purchases

The one in-app purchase, Remove Ads, is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Google Play. We store only a single yes/no flag - whether you've bought it - so the app knows to stop requesting ads. That flag lives in the same on-device storage described in Section 2, and is separately re-confirmed against your Google Play purchase history each time the app starts, so the purchase survives reinstalls.

5. Your choices

  • Turn off ad personalization at the device level, in your Google Account's Data & Privacy settings.
  • Remove ads entirely with the one-time in-app purchase, from the app's Settings screen.
  • Reset your advertising ID in your device's system settings at any time.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app, which removes all local data immediately.

6. Children's privacy

Overripe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data through the third-party services above, please contact us and we'll help you address it through Google's own tools.

7. Data retention

We don't operate a database of our own, so we don't decide retention periods for analytics or advertising data - that's governed by Firebase's and AdMob's own retention settings, documented in Google's Privacy Policy. On-device data (Section 2) is retained for as long as the app remains installed.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Overripe after a change means you accept the update.
